How it works

  1. 1JE records the schoolAt the school, the JE takes the photo gallery and before photos of each proposed work, with the estimated amount. Photos work only inside the school's 300 m boundary.
  2. 2District reviewsThe district office sees every school's submission and photos, block-wise and category-wise, and follows up on pending schools.
  3. 3Directorate prioritisesThe Directorate marks each work Priority or Non-priority and tracks works and amounts across all 38 districts.
